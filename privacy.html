Privacy Policy

TBFlog, by ToBeFit Ultimate Coaching · Last updated 9 September 2026

TBFlog is a training-log app used by coaches and their athletes. This policy explains what information the app collects, why it is collected, who can see it, and how you can get it deleted. It applies to the TBFlog website and to the TBFlog iOS and Android apps, which show the same service.

The short version: we collect the account details you give us and the training data you record in the app. Your coach can see your training data — that is the point of the app. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and you can permanently delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

ToBeFit Ultimate Coaching is the data controller for information collected through TBFlog. For any privacy question or request, contact tobefitcoach@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and password, used to sign in. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider — we never see or store them in readable form.
  • Your name.
  • Whether your account is a coach account or an athlete account.
  • A profile photo, if you choose to upload one.

Training and activity data

  • The training programmes assigned to you, and the exercises within them.
  • What you actually completed: sets, repetitions, weights, times, dates, and your own notes.
  • Workout sessions, including when you started and finished.
  • Effort and difficulty ratings you record, including any follow-up you give about pain or injury when you report a very hard session.
  • Mobility and stretching preferences.
  • Tournaments or competitions you add, with their dates and how important you mark them.

Messages and questionnaires

  • Messages exchanged between you and your coach inside the app.
  • Your answers to forms and questionnaires your coach sends you, including free-text answers and rating-scale answers.

Notifications

  • If you enable push notifications, we store the notification token your browser or device issues. This identifies the device to the push service; it does not identify you personally to us beyond linking to your account.
  • Your notification preferences, such as whether you want a weekly recap.

What we do not collect

TBFlog does not collect your location, does not access your contacts, does not use advertising identifiers, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

3. Why we use it

We use the information above only to run the service:

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show your training programme and record what you complete.
  • To let your coach review your training, adjust your programme, and message you.
  • To send the notifications you have asked for.
  • To diagnose faults and keep the service working.

We do not sell your personal data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Who can see your data

Your coach

If you are an athlete, the coach who you are linked to can see your profile, your assigned programme, your logged training, your effort ratings and notes, your questionnaire answers, your tournaments, and your messages with them. This is the core function of the app. Coaches can only see the athletes linked to their own account.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides our database, authentication, file storage, and server functions. Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure and processed on our instructions.
  • Push notification services operated by Apple, Google, and Mozilla deliver notifications to your device when you enable them. They receive the notification content and device token needed to deliver it.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's web files. It does not receive your account or training data.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to do so.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and training data for as long as your account exists, so that your training history stays available to you and your coach. If you delete your account, the data described in section 6 is removed immediately and permanently. Backups held by our hosting provider may retain copies for a short period before they are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account from inside the app: open Profile, scroll to the bottom, and choose Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm, because this cannot be undone.

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your login and account record.
  • Your name, profile details, and profile photo.
  • Your logged training, sets, sessions, effort ratings, and notes.
  • Your tournaments and mobility preferences.
  • Your questionnaire answers and form assignments.
  • Your messages with your coach.
  • Your notification tokens and preferences.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be reversed. If you would rather ask us to do it for you, email tobefitcoach@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will action it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, and in particular if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Have your data deleted (see section 6).
  • Object to or restrict how we use your data.
  • Withdraw consent for notifications at any time, by disabling them in the app.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email tobefitcoach@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Supabase infrastructure with row-level access rules, so that accounts can only read the records they are entitled to. Passwords are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical measures to protect your information.

9. Children

TBFlog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. Where an athlete is a minor, the account should be set up and supervised by a parent or guardian, who can contact us at any time to review or delete that data. If you believe a child has an account without appropriate consent, contact us and we will remove it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process and store data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, they do so under the safeguards required by applicable data protection law.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and where the change is significant we will tell you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: tobefitcoach@gmail.com
