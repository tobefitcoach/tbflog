Week strip options

Four ways to replace the current status dots on the athlete home screen's week strip, using the same 7-day row and real card styling. Monday is finished, Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday have something scheduled (Wednesday has two things), Friday/Saturday are open, Sunday just has a tournament.

A Bigger type icons

Dots become small real icons per item - a barbell, a stopwatch, a runner, a stretch figure, a medal - at roughly 2x today's dot size. You can tell what's scheduled without tapping in.
